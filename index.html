ABOUT THIS PROJECT

A language worth preserving.

Zomi Dictionary is a research and heritage initiative focused on Tedim Zomi. This website opens the English–Zomi Dictionary 2.0 APK locally; it does not redistribute the APK's database. English entries are indexed by their headword and Tedim content by the full explanation text. A match inside an explanation does not necessarily represent a standalone dictionary headword.
